Secret Vault VERSION keeps your passwords, security codes, cards and keys in one encrypted file on this Mac.

This installs:

  • Secret Vault.app in Applications;
  • the vault, vaultd and vault-gpg commands in /usr/local/bin;
  • a launch agent that starts vaultd, the background program that holds your unlocked vault, when you sign in.

If you already have Secret Vault, this upgrades it. Your vault and settings are kept.

Status: not audited. Do not trust the vault with real secrets yet.
